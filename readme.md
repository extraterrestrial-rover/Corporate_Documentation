# Team Inferno Corporate & Management

* **Brand Evolution:** Transitioned from a casual aesthetic to a formal, minimalistic brand identity to better position the team with sponsors and judges.

* **Core Projects:** Tracks the conceptualization of the "Inferno Exchange" and the evolution of our PIMA (Project Implementation and Management Assessment) and BPP (Business and Partnership Plan) strategies.

## Strategic Roadmap for Next Cycle

* **Early Preparation:** Initiating PIMA and BPP planning significantly earlier to easily meet all submission deadlines.

* **Domain-Based Workflow:** Restructuring task allocation to a domain-based model to maximize team efficiency and output quality.

* **Asset Documentation Protocol:** Implementing a strict checklist for all physical deliverables (standees, visiting cards, brochures, stickers) to ensure timely and organized procurement.

* **Visual Identity Update:** Retaining our formal, minimalistic logo while updating team jersey designs to align with current professional trends.
